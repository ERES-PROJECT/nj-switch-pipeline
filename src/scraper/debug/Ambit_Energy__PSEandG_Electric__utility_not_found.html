Time of Use (TOU)  plans offer on peak and off peak rates. On peak rates occur during weekdays from 12:00 Noon to 8PM. Off peak rates occur all other times of the day.


To qualify for this plan, United Illuminating (UI) requires you to have a smart electric meter. Look at page 1 of your invoice by the "Trans and Dist Rate". If you see an "RT", "GST", or "SL-GST" code, you are eligible for the TOU plan and may enroll now. If you do not have one of these UI codes, please either select a different Ambit Plan or stop enrollment, contact UI at (800) 722 5584 and request a TOU compatible meter.
