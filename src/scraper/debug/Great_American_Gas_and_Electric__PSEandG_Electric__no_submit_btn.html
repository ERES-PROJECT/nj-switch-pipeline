Keeping a home up and running is not always easy. When a toilet or water heater breaks down, it can be difficult to come up with the money to get them fixed. And finding a trustworthy service pro can be hard too.

Then there are power surges that can damage appliances and electronics, causing additional repair and replacement costs that can be very expensive.  That’s what a home protection plan from Cinch is all about:  making it simple for customers to get the help they need at a price they can afford.

HOW A CINCH HOME PROTECTION PLAN WORKS

  1. When a covered item breaks down, customers can request service online or by phone.
  2. We’ll find a pre-screened service pro in their area to fix the item.
  3. The service pro will repair the item (if it can’t be repaired, it will be replaced).
  4. The customer pays a deductible and the repair is guaranteed for 180 days (non-covered charges may apply).

Keeping a home up and running is not always easy.  When cooling or heating systems break down,it can be difficult to come up with the money to get them fixed.  And finding a trustworthy service pro can be hard too.

Then there are power surges that can damage appliances and electronics, causing additional repair and replacement costs that can be very expensive.  That’s what a home protection plan from Cinch is all about:  making it simple for customers to get the help they need at a price they can afford.

HOW A CINCH HOME PROTECTION PLAN WORKS

  1. When a covered item breaks down, service can be requested anytime online or by phone and the preset deductible is collected.
  2. We’ll find a pre-screened service pro in their area to fix the item.
  3. The service pro will repair the item (if it can’t be repaired, it will be replaced).
  4. The customer pays a deductible and the repair is guaranteed for 180 days (non-covered charges may apply).
  5. For SURGE PROTECTION, customers can repair or replace the item however they choose. They then complete and submit a reimbursement form. Once the claim is approved, they will receive a check for costs incurred, up to their coverage limit, within 6 weeks.
